## Histology fields (BCSS)

`bcss_*.jpg` are 896 x 896 pixel crops (0.25 microns per pixel) of the
colour-normalised regions of interest of the BCSS dataset, from TCGA breast
cancer slides; the file name gives the slide and the crop's offset within the
region. BCSS is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

> Amgad M, Elfandy H, Hussein H, et al. Structured crowdsourcing enables
> convolutional segmentation of histology images. *Bioinformatics*
> 35(18):3461–3467, 2019. doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btz083

The crops were saved as JPEG (quality 92). They come from the reporting
patients of the evaluation and are shown for illustration only.
